Privacy

BetterBuwis does not need your TIN.

The estimator is designed to work in your browser. The amounts you enter are not sent to BetterBuwis by the calculator code.

Information we do not request

  • Taxpayer Identification Numbers
  • Government IDs or account passwords
  • Full tax returns, invoices, or registration certificates
  • Employer, client, or bank-account details

Analytics

BetterBuwis uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate use, such as page visits, estimator completions, and the general taxpayer category selected. The site does not intentionally send the peso amounts entered into the estimator to analytics. IP anonymization is requested in the site configuration, subject to Google’s service behavior and policies.

Email

Messages sent to hello@betterbuwis.com arrive in the operator’s Gmail account. Email only what is necessary. Do not attach IDs, credentials, or full tax records. We may retain correspondence as needed to answer, document corrections, or protect the site, then delete it when no longer needed.

Future CPA referrals

BetterBuwis is not presently operating a paid CPA marketplace on this revision. Before any referral program is activated, the site will ask for explicit consent, explain what information will be shared, identify the purpose, and disclose whether BetterBuwis may receive a fee.

External websites

Links to BIR, Lawphil, payment providers, or other sites are governed by those sites’ own privacy and security practices.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests concerning an email you sent, contact hello@betterbuwis.com.

Effective: 13 July 2026.
